## Findings

- Producing a correct change is close across harnesses: with Luna 80–93% lenient, with nano 53–68% (Codex 15%).
- Ending cleanly is what separates them. OpenCode and mini-swe-agent always end with their own completion protocol, so strict equals lenient. The loops with an explicit `done` tool (the product's AI SDK loop, both OpenAI Agents SDK arms, AI SDK over Responses, pi) often stop with a message instead: with nano the product's loop never called `done` although 40 of its 60 changes passed.
- Codex depends on a strong model: with nano it made no change in 42 of 60 attempts and cost three to six times more per attempt.
- mini-swe-agent changed a test file in 5 of its Luna attempts, the most of any harness (the change rules reject it).
- workspace-money-units was passed once in 48 attempts across every harness and model: agents fix the failing workspace and miss the second consumer the API change broke, which no visible test covers.
- Follow-up: the product now ends an attempt done when the model stops after changing files and every failing step's own script then passes in the box (commit 21f3a2e); round 3 re-runs the two AI SDK arms with it.
